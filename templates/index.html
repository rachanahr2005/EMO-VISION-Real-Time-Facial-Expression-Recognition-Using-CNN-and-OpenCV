EMO VISION

EMO VISION is a deep learning based facial expression recognition system that combines Convolutional Neural Networks with OpenCV to analyze facial images in real time.

The system detects a face, preprocesses the facial region into a 48 × 48 grayscale image, and classifies it into one of seven supported facial expression categories.
